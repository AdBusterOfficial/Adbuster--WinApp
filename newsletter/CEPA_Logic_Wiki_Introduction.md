# Introducing the CEPA Logic Wiki

I am pleased to share the **CEPA Logic Wiki**, a growing knowledge base dedicated to Contextual Event Pattern Analysis.

The Wiki brings together the foundations of the methodology, its design principles, analytical approach, decision models, case studies, glossary, and research directions.

At the heart of CEPA Logic is a simple principle:

**Context Before Action.**

Detecting an event is only the beginning. Understanding its context, recognising patterns, and deciding whether a response is justified are equally important parts of the analytical process.

The Wiki also distinguishes between the CEPA Logic methodology and its experimental implementations. The publicly available AdBuster 2.0 remains a limited prototype, while AdBuster PRO is a separate laboratory and R&D project.

This documentation is an ongoing effort to explain the ideas behind CEPA Logic, clarify its scope, and provide a foundation for further exploration.

🌍 [Explore the CEPA Logic Wiki →](https://github.com/AdBusterOfficial/Adbuster--WinApp/wiki)

Thank you to everyone who takes an interest in this work. Feedback and thoughtful discussion are welcome.
