
# 🎧 Acoustic Feature Analysis Report (ADS vs NORMAL)

A concise acoustic comparison between advertisement audio (ADS) and regular audio content (NORMAL), supporting behavioural interpretation inside CEPA Logic PRO.

## 🧩 Summary

- 29‑feature acoustic fingerprint (MFCC, Chroma, Centroid, Bandwidth, RMS, ZCR)  
- Clear acoustic differences between ADS and NORMAL  
- ADS: brighter, wider spectrum, more transients, stronger compression artifacts  
- NORMAL: natural tonal structure, smooth MFCC profile  
- Verified across ADS, NORMAL, MUSIC, TALK  
- Stable cooperation between AdBuster PRO (ML + IR stabilizer) and CEPA Logic PRO (behaviour engine)

## 🎯 Purpose

This Labs entry documents the acoustic foundations used by CEPA Logic PRO to interpret audio context and maintain consistent behavioural responses during ADS/NORMAL transitions.
