  <h2 style="color:#D4AF37; font-size:30px; margin-bottom:15px; text-align:center;">
    What is Broadlink?
  </h2>

  <p style="font-size:17px; color:#DDD;">
    Broadlink is a compact smart device that works as a modern wireless remote control for your TV and audio equipment.
    Instead of pressing buttons on a physical remote, Broadlink sends the same infrared (IR) signals automatically through
    your home Wi‑Fi network. Every TV reacts to IR signals, and Broadlink can reproduce them exactly, without pointing a
    remote at the screen or even being in the same room.
  </p>

  <p style="font-size:17px; color:#DDD;">
    Broadlink receives a command over Wi‑Fi, converts it into an IR signal, and sends it to your TV, soundbar, receiver,
    or set‑top box. The device responds as if you pressed a button on the remote – for example <b style="color:#D4AF37;">Volume Down</b>,
    <b style="color:#D4AF37;">Volume Up</b>, or <b style="color:#D4AF37;">Mute</b>. Broadlink does not require internet access, does not
    require a cloud account, and operates fully inside your local home network. This makes it fast, reliable, and fully
    independent from external servers.
  </p>

  <p style="font-size:17px; color:#DDD;">
    Broadlink can control any device that uses an IR remote, including TVs, set‑top boxes, soundbars, AV receivers,
    projectors, and many other home entertainment devices. In simple terms, Broadlink is an intelligent Wi‑Fi remote
    that can automatically press the buttons for you, without needing to touch your physical remote.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color:#D4AF37; font-size:24px; margin-top:30px;">
    How Broadlink Works
  </h3>

  <p style="font-size:17px; color:#DDD;">
    Broadlink connects to your home Wi‑Fi network. When it receives a command, it instantly sends the matching IR signal
    to your device. Because it uses Wi‑Fi, Broadlink does not need to be in front of the TV or pointed at it. As long as
    the Broadlink device has a clear line of sight to the TV’s IR receiver, it can control it automatically.
  </p>

  <p style="font-size:17px; color:#DDD;">
    Everything happens locally, which means:
    <br>• <span style="color:#D4AF37;">no internet required</span>
    <br>• <span style="color:#D4AF37;">no cloud communication</span>
    <br>• <span style="color:#D4AF37;">no delays</span>
    <br>• <span style="color:#D4AF37;">no privacy concerns</span>
  </p>

  <h3 style="color:#D4AF37; font-size:24px; margin-top:30px;">
    Broadlink Models Compatible with AdBuster
  </h3>

  <p style="font-size:17px; color:#DDD;">
    AdBuster works with any Broadlink device that supports <b style="color:#D4AF37;">IR (infrared)</b> and operates in
    <b style="color:#D4AF37;">local LAN mode</b>. These devices can send standard IR commands such as volume control,
    mute, and other audio‑related functions.
  </p>

  <ul style="font-size:17px; color:#EEE; margin-left:20px;">
    <li><b style="color:#D4AF37;">Broadlink RM4 Mini (RM4C Mini)</b></li>
    <li><b style="color:#D4AF37;">Broadlink RM4 Pro</b></li>
    <li><b style="color:#D4AF37;">Broadlink RM Mini 3 (RM3 Mini)</b></li>
    <li><b style="color:#D4AF37;">Broadlink RM Pro+</b></li>
    <li><b style="color:#D4AF37;">Broadlink RM4C Pro</b></li>
    <li><b style="color:#D4AF37;">Broadlink RM4 TV Mate</b> (if IR is supported)</li>
  </ul>

  <p style="font-size:17px; color:#DDD; margin-top:10px;">
    <b style="color:#D4AF37;">Older models that also work:</b> RM2 Pro, RM2 Home, RM2 Mini.
  </p>

  <p style="font-size:17px; color:#DDD; margin-top:10px;">
    <b style="color:#D4AF37;">Not supported:</b> RF‑only devices, smart plugs, sensors, switches, and cloud‑only models.
